Business

A film should not only look good. It should have a job to do.

Since 2017 I have also been running the business behind the work. At VIVEN AG I built and led an inbound-driven approach to new business, from positioning and content through client conversations and proposals to negotiation and closing. I then embedded the process in the team so it could scale beyond me.

That shaped how I think about production. Whether the goal is to build a brand, explain a product, attract talent or support a campaign, I look at every piece of content in the context of the marketing and business objective behind it.

How I work

I start with the objective, not the format.

What does the content need to achieve? From there I shape the creative approach, build the right team and lead the production through to final delivery, so the result works creatively, operationally and commercially.

  1. Concept & development

    Goal, audience, story and format.

  2. Producing

    Team, locations, schedule, suppliers and budget.

  3. Shoot

    Leading on set, with clear decisions and calm communication.

  4. Post & delivery

    Edit, sound, colour, versions, localisation and final delivery.

About

Creative eye. Producer’s discipline.

I’m Sebastian, a creative production lead and executive producer based in Zürich.

I started behind the camera in 2009 and worked across concept, producing, directing, cinematography, editing and colour. Even as my role moved into production leadership, the filmmaker’s perspective stayed at the centre of how I work.

Born in Buenos Aires and based in Zürich, I work across cultures and languages. Off set, I’m usually outdoors, behind a camera or with a book.
