This website is a personal profile. It sets no cookies, shows no ads and does not track you. Here is exactly what happens when you visit.

Who is responsible

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA). Like any web host, Netlify automatically processes technical data when a page is requested, such as IP address, date and time, the requested page, browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure, and it is not used to identify or profile visitors. Netlify may process this data outside Switzerland, including in the USA, on the basis of appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Analytics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless service from Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). It counts page views anonymously and in aggregate (for example the page visited, the referring site and the country). It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device and does not follow you across other websites.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Fonts and videos

All fonts and videos are hosted on this website itself. Your browser makes no connection to Google Fonts, YouTube, Vimeo or any other third party to display them.

If you contact me

If you write to me by email or call me, I use your message and contact details only to reply to you. I do not add you to any mailing list and do not pass your data on to anyone.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). To exercise your rights, just write to me.
